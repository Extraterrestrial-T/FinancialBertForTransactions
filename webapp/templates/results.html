Two uses for the learned account embedding

The model is evaluated on account-disjoint snapshots. The test split was not used for tokenizer fitting, MLM training, adapter fitting, rank selection or threshold selection.

Why only test accounts appear in the lab

The old lab listed train, validation and test accounts because it was built as a data explorer. That was confusing for a public demonstration. The lab now samples only the final test split, so its observed future outcomes are proper held-out examples. Training accounts are still necessary inside the training pipeline: they provide the MLM data and downstream labels used to fit weights, but they should not be presented as evidence of generalisation.

Metrics are exploratory results from the committed Czech-bank experiment.
